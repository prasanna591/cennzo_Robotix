--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
0. HOW TO USE THIS DOCUMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every slot below lists: WHERE it appears, WHAT it must communicate, exact ASPECT
RATIO (+ recommended pixel size), COMPOSITION guidance, TONE & LIGHTING, and a
ready-to-paste GENERATION PROMPT (Midjourney syntax, with a generic variant for
DALL·E / SDXL / Flux / Ideogram).

Golden rules:
• Generate the GLOBAL CHARACTER SHEET first (Section 2), then reuse it as a
  character/seed reference for every WAFEE appearance so the robot looks like
  ONE machine across the whole site.
• All images are displayed inside dark UI chrome on a near-black page. Images
  must be dark-to-midtone with bright focal subjects so they sit naturally in
  the layout. Avoid pure-white backgrounds anywhere.
• Every frame is labelled "Concept" in the UI — imagery should feel like
  credible engineering visualization, NOT consumer marketing gloss and NOT
  sci-fi movie VFX. Think: Boston Dynamics documentation shot meets Apple
  product film, shot by an industrial photographer.

1. GLOBAL ART DIRECTION (applies to ALL images)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PALETTE
  Base/environment ....... graphite #16161A, ink #0B1220, void near-black #0A0A0C
  Highlights/text ........ bone #F5F7FB, cool steel greys
  Primary accent ......... electric blue #1565EF (used sparingly: status LEDs,
                           holographic overlays, rim light)
  Secondary accents ...... violet #7C3AED (energy/data), teal (sensor scans),
                           signal amber #F59E0B (warning/servo glow)
  Rule: max ~10% accent color per frame; environment stays desaturated.

MOOD
  Cinematic industrial realism · quiet confidence · "built for hard places" ·
  documentary-product-film hybrid · precision engineering · calm danger.

LIGHTING LANGUAGE (pick per slot, stay consistent site-wide)
  • Soft top-light studio gradient on dark seamless backdrop (product shots)
  • Hard practical work-lights / sodium or arc-lamp pools (industrial floors)
  • Cold blue rim light + warm amber fill for drama (hero/close-ups)
  • Volumetric haze at low density — depth without fog-wall clichés

CAMERA LANGUAGE
  Full-frame look, 35–85mm equivalents, f/2.8–f/8, shallow-ish DOF only for
  close-ups; wide plates use deep focus. Subtle handheld-documentary energy is
  OK; no fisheye, no tilt-shift toy effect, no HDR halos.

TEXTURE & MATERIAL TRUTH
  Brushed aluminium, matte composite shell, visible fasteners, cable looms,
  machined actuator knuckles, fine machining marks, dust in hazardous scenes.
  No chrome-liquid "terminator" surfaces, no glossy toy plastic.

WAFEE CHARACTER SHEET — THE ROBOT (define once, reference forever)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WAFEE is a full-body humanoid utility robot:
  Height ~1.75 m human-scale. Proportions: slightly broad shoulders, compact
  torso, segmented spine channel; head = smooth rounded visor slab (no face,
  no eyes) with a single horizontal sensor slit that glows teal/blue; neck
  ring with port details.
  Skin/shell: two-tone matte — bone-white composite plates over graphite
  under-frame; exposed dark actuator joints at shoulders/elbows/hips/knees
  with thin amber status rings; cable looms along forearms and calves;
  rectangular backpack power core with blue LED strip; four-finger hands,
  articulated and mechanical (not gloves).
  Feet: wide split-toe bipedal gripper feet. Overall read: "industrial
  astronaut", friendly-mechanical, zero humanoid-face uncanny valley.

NEGATIVE PROMPT (append everywhere applicable)
  "no text, no watermark, no logo, no human faces, no crowd, no glossy plastic
  toy look, no sci-fi neon cyberpunk city, no lens flare spam, no oversaturated
  colors, no white background, no cartoon, no extra limbs, no distorted hands"

QUALITY TAGS (append to generic prompts)
  "photorealistic, cinematic lighting, high detail, 8k product photography,
  industrial design portfolio, physically based materials"

MIDJOURNEY SETUP
  • Use --v 6.1 (or newer), --style raw for realism.
  • Aspect flags per slot: --ar 21:9, --ar 16:9, --ar 4:3.
  • After approving the character sheet image, add --cref <sheetURL> --cw 60
    to every WAFEE prompt for consistency. Keep --sref of your best-approved
    plate (--sref <url> --sw 40) to lock grading across slots.
  • Upscale to max, then export per Section 4.
